A LITTLE ABOUT ME

oh✳

Always developing.

It started with
a love of image making.

I’m Owen Holbrook, a Digital Multimedia student at Derby College with a particular interest in graphic design and image editing.

I discovered photo and image editing during my GCSEs in 2023. That curiosity led me to study digital multimedia, where I’m developing my visual ideas and expanding the ways I work with images.

Music is a big influence. Reinterpreting album covers has given me a way to connect something personal with design, exploring how a change in style can give familiar imagery a different character.
